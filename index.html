03 / About

About

I’m a graphic designer based in Chattogram, Bangladesh, working with brands across industries. Since January 2025 I’ve been the in-house graphic designer at AR Group of Companies, where I build and maintain brand identity (logos, color palettes and typography) across the group’s divisions, working closely with the marketing, branding and product teams.

Alongside my full-time role, I take on freelance projects for companies of all kinds, working remotely with clients on branding, product labels, packaging, advertising, and print and digital campaigns. Whether a product is new or established, I aim for clear, consistent design that is easy to notice and easy to trust. Outside client work I also create football and festival posters.
